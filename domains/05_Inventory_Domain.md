# Inventory & Materials Management Domain

## 1. Overview

The Inventory & Materials Management Domain is responsible for tracking, controlling, and maintaining every physical inventory movement within the manufacturing facility.

It serves as the central inventory ledger of the ERP.

Every stock movement generated by Procurement, Manufacturing, Warehouse, or Export must pass through this domain.

Inventory does not create business transactions.

Instead, it records inventory movements generated by other operational domains.

---

# 2. Business Objectives

The Inventory Domain shall

- Maintain accurate inventory balances.
- Track inventory movements.
- Record complete stock history.
- Support inventory valuation.
- Prevent negative inventory.
- Support lot and batch traceability.
- Maintain warehouse balances.
- Provide inventory information to Manufacturing.

# 7. Business Rules

1. Inventory cannot become negative.

2. Expired materials cannot be issued.

3. Reserved inventory cannot be transferred.

4. Batch-controlled items require batch numbers.

5. Finished goods require production batch reference.

6. Goods cannot bypass Quality Inspection.

7. Every movement creates a stock movement record.

8. Inventory adjustments require approval.
